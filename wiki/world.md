## After Chapter 2

### The Revolution
- Has structure and cells; this cell has at least six people plus Varo
- Has been watching mine exits for escapees as an intelligence-gathering operation; Varo's assignment for five months
- Uses Morthael's name constantly and deliberately — as a tool to transform fear into a target
- Specifically wants absorbers — has theorized about them from "old texts" before seeing one in person
- The safe house: cut-stone passage off a natural cave, reinforced with lumber; built toward something, not away
- The revolution refers to her as *absorber* — this is now her functional identity within their structure

### Magic System — Additions from Ch. 2
- **Deliberate absorption:** Slower than involuntary; she feels the approach before entry; she can feel herself and the dead person simultaneously for ~2 seconds before full entry
- **Emotional content:** Skills are inseparable from the emotional texture of the person — absorbing Senna means absorbing her joy; cannot separate the craft from the crafter
- **Cost scaling:** Ch. 2 absorption cost less than Ch. 1 (shorter duration? smaller crystal?); cost appears proportional but exact formula unknown
- **Morthael's awareness:** He may already sense a disturbance in collection patterns — a gap where a life should have been absorbed and wasn't (unconfirmed; Jemisin draft only)

### Geography
- The revolution's safe house: approximately one hour's walk from the mine exit, downhill, then underground via a natural cave system

## After Chapter 1

### Magic System: Crystal Absorption
- **Trigger:** Skin contact with light dispersed from a broken memory crystal
- **Effect:** Involuntary absorption of the dead person's complete memories, skills, and personality for ~4 seconds
- **Limit:** Duration is brief and non-renewable — the borrowed knowledge fades; you cannot choose what to retain
- **Cost:** Physical aging in the absorber — amount unknown but visible; skin thins, lines deepen (protagonist's hands aged after one 4-second absorption)
- **Normal behavior:** Crystals break occasionally; dispersed light is theoretically inert for ordinary people — the protagonist is the exception

### The Memory Mines
- Underground facilities where harvested memory crystals are sorted, wrapped in ash-cloth, and shipped upward to Morthael's collectors
- Workers hauling crates: the lowest tier; brought in as children, never leave
- Rules: no dropping crystals, no looking at them too long, eyes down when incidents occur
- Self-policing: workers watch each other; Harvesters only need to respond, not surveil
- Tunnel six north wall, third bracket: a structurally weak section exploited by the protagonist for escape; natural fault passage leads to the surface (now broken open)

### Factions / Powers
- **Morthael** — the god-king; cannot die; fueled by harvested memories; his collectors receive sorted crystals; never appears directly but his weight is felt throughout
- **Harvesters** — his enforcement class; respond to crystal incidents; workers taken by them are never returned
- **Collectors** — upper-level agents who receive sorted crystals for Morthael

### Geography / World State
- The outside above the mines: a hillside; ash falls from a bone-pale sky (unknown whether ash or snow)
- The bridge at the river Selt: famous structure built by Aldric Vann; still standing sixty years after his death
- The world above the mines is unknown to the protagonist — she has no map
