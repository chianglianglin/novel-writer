## Chapter 2

- **Ch. 2 / The Contact** — After half a day walking, Varo steps out of the ash. He identifies her aged hands immediately — "both of them" — and tells her he's been watching that exit for five months. He is not a hero; he is practical and assembling intelligence.
- **Ch. 2 / The Name** — Varo says Morthael's name aloud for the first time in her hearing. Says it three times deliberately, as demonstration: "You say it enough and it stops being a fear and starts being a target."
- **Ch. 2 / The First Deliberate Absorption** — Protagonist opens the stolen crystal (Senna, a forger/lockpick). First controlled absorption: she feels the approach, chooses to enter, manages it. Gains lockpick and forgery skills. Absorbs Senna's joy — the most foreign thing she has ever felt. Four seconds. Cost: smaller but real aging of hands. Senna's skill and happiness both fade.
- **Ch. 2 / The Safe House** — Varo leads her underground to the revolution's cut-stone safe house. Six people inside; they look up at her with the concentrated attention of a cause acquiring its missing piece. She is called *absorber* — twice. She eats. She doesn't choose yet.

## Chapter 1

- **Ch. 1 / The Breaking** — Netta drops her crate in tunnel four; a crystal breaks. Protagonist braces her hand on the floor and accidentally makes skin contact with the dispersing crystal light. Involuntarily absorbs 43 years of Aldric Vann's memories for ~4 seconds. Nobody sees.
- **Ch. 1 / The Discovery** — After the absorption fades, protagonist notices her hands have aged (thinner skin, deeper lines). Realizes something was taken as well as given. Does not yet understand the mechanism.
- **Ch. 1 / The Escape** — Protagonist steals one intact pale-green crystal from reserve bins. Uses Aldric's borrowed masonry knowledge to identify and push out a structurally weak wall panel in tunnel six (north wall, third bracket). Climbs a natural passage to the surface.
- **Ch. 1 / The Surface** — Protagonist emerges onto a hillside in open air for the first time. Ash falls from a pale sky. She has no map, no food, no direction. Harvesters will find the broken wall by nightfall.
