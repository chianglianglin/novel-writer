## After Chapter 2

- **Varo** — Revolution contact, ~30. Mine-built (escaped from similar work years ago). Has been watching the mine exits for escapees for five months; found four others before her (three recaptured, one survived — Coll). The first person to use Morthael's name in her presence. Practical, suspicious, not warm. Makes noise when approaching so people have time to prepare. Identified her aged hands instantly as something new.
- **Senna** — Dead lockpick and forger, 23 when she died (19 years before Ch. 2). The crystal the protagonist stole from the reserve bins. Her skill: forging documents and picking locks. Her distinguishing trait: she was happy — structurally glad, the load-bearing element of her. The protagonist absorbs her deliberately in Ch. 2; the emotional cost is losing Senna's joy.
- **Coll** — One of four mine escapees Varo found; the only one besides the protagonist to survive. Mentioned briefly; has not appeared directly.
- **The revolution cell** — Six people in the safe house; named collectively as a presence; none individuated yet.

## After Chapter 1

- **Protagonist (unnamed)** — Young woman, ~16, raised in the memory mines since age 4-5. No knowledge of the outside world. Discovered she can involuntarily absorb memory crystals on skin contact. Escaped the mines using a dead mason's borrowed skill. Carries one intact crystal. Her hands have aged slightly from the absorption.
- **Aldric Vann** — Dead master stonemason, third order. Built the bridge at the river Selt. Wife: Betha (smelled of pine resin, always burned the first batch of bread). Two sons. Bad left knee. Died alone on a winter road later named for someone else. His crystal was the one broken in Ch. 1.
- **Netta** — Mine worker. Dropped her crate in Ch. 1, triggering the incident. Taken by Harvesters; not returned.
- **The Foreman** — Authority figure in the mines, keeps a ledger. Reacts to incidents by looking up and then writing.
- **Harvesters** — Morthael's enforcement class. Respond to crystal-breaking incidents. Workers taken by Harvesters are never seen again.
