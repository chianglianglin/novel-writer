# Cormac McCarthy Draft — Chapter 2

**Confidence:** 86%

**Style Notes:** No quotation marks, no apostrophes throughout; Morthael named three times deliberately — each repetition a demonstration of the principle being stated; Senna's joy rendered as a stark anomaly; "a different kind of stone" applies McCarthy's landscape-as-moral-force to the safe house; the closing admits she's been thinking about Senna despite saying she won't.

She had been walking since the day before and she was tired in the way that has nothing to do with sleep and she did not know the country or which way was safe or whether safe was a thing the country contained.

The ash came down and settled on her clothes and her hair and did not stop. The sky was the same sky it had been when she came out of the ground. She did not know where she was going. She had the crystal in her pocket pressing against her ribs and she had not used it yet because she did not know what was in it and she wanted to know before she needed to know but she did not know when needing would come.

The man came out of the grey.

She stopped. He stopped. He was looking at her hands.

He was not a Harvester. He was mine-built but not a Harvester. She could read the difference the way you read the difference between two kinds of stone that look the same but are not.

Both of them he said.

She did not move.

I watched that exit for five months he said. He said it the way you say a thing when you have been waiting a long time to say it and now the time has come and the thing is being said. I found four others. Three went back. One didnt.

He looked at her hands again.

None of them had that he said.

She put her hands in her pockets.

What did you absorb he said.

She told him. He listened. He asked how long she had held it. She said it was gone in minutes. He said I thought so and then he said something she had not heard before. He said the name.

Morthael.

She heard it land. She had heard it before somewhere in the mines in the voices of people who did not finish the word and she had always known the shape of the silence around it but she had not heard it finished. Not spoken to completion. Not said the way this man said it which was the way you say something you say every day until the weight of it is just weight and not fear.

We say it he said. We say it because if you dont say it it gets bigger. He said the name again. Morthael. The god who cannot die. He said it a third time as if to demonstrate that a word said enough times becomes only a word. Thats who made the mines he said. Thats who we intend to end.

She took out the crystal.

He watched her. He did not reach for it. He was not afraid of it. He watched the way you watch a thing you have been told about and are now seeing for the first time and need to reconcile the telling with the thing.

She pressed it against her palm.

The deliberate was different from the accident. In the accident it came all at once without warning. Deliberate she felt the approach. She felt herself and the dead person both present for a moment before the dead person took everything.

The woman had been a forger. Her name was Senna and she was twenty three when she died and she had been happy which was the strangest thing about her because it came in with the skill and could not be separated from it and happiness was not something she had any framework for. Senna made documents and opened locks and was glad about it. She was glad in the morning and glad at night and glad when the work was going well and glad when the work was not going well because at least the work was interesting. She was glad in the specific way of someone who has not yet been given a reason not to be.

Four seconds.

Then she was herself again on the slope in the ash and her hands showed what they always showed now which was the cost.

Forger she said. Lockpick.

He nodded. He did not say anything for a moment. Then he said and it costs you.

She held her hands out. He looked at them.

Then he turned and said come with me.

She followed him because the alternative was the hillside and the dark and the Harvesters working down from the mine entrance. She did not tell herself it was more than that. She kept it simple in the way she had always kept things. It was the hillside or this man and the hillside was not survivable alone and so she walked behind him in the ash with her hands in her pockets and did not think about Senna.

She thought about Senna.

They went underground again. The passage was different. Cut stone but chosen stone. Made by people with a reason. She knew the difference in stone by now.

Six people in a room lit by lamps. They looked up when she came in and the looking was a particular kind of looking which was the looking of people who have decided something about you before you arrive. Someone put food in front of her. She ate it.

The man whose name was Varo spoke to the others and she heard the word absorber twice. She heard the way it moved through the room the way a word moves when it means something that has been waited for.

She looked at her hands in the lamplight. The lines in them that hadnt been there yesterday. The skin at her knuckles thin as the skin on still water.

She had come out of the mines and walked into the ash and now she was underground again in a room full of people who had already decided she mattered. The mines had never decided she mattered. They had decided she was useful which was not the same thing and she had known the difference even before she had words for it.

These people had decided she mattered. This was better and it was also its own kind of weight which was something she had not expected. She had thought mattered would feel like release. It felt like a different kind of stone.

She ate her food. Nobody had asked her anything yet.

She did not know what she would say when they did.
